# Enemy concepts — first visual exploration

Generated with the built-in Imagegen tool. These are exploratory concept sheets, not final canon, production models, or implemented gameplay. Google Docs was not changed in this request.

All four are supernatural constructs with no living human hidden inside. Their humanlike silhouettes express the missing-person story while keeping humans in Camp visually distinct. The fictional return ritual belongs to Don Lap; these are not depictions of real protective spirits or religious beings.

## Final prompt set / design directions

Shared direction: stylized-concept, semi-realistic production character sheet for Project Midnight in fictional Don Lap, northeastern Thailand. Readable full-body three-quarter, supplementary side/back views and material details; neutral warm-grey studio backdrop; realistic worn materials and restrained colours. No gore, injured-human/zombie appearance, trapped people, sacred deity iconography, or copied franchise designs.

- **searcher-v1.png / ผู้ค้นหา — THE SEARCHER:** Endless searching. Tall narrow humanoid, long arms nearly touching ground, hollow aged wooden head with central flashlight aperture, no eyes or face. Worn field jacket over visibly empty framework. Dusty khaki, charcoal wood and tiny amber beam. Show ground scanning, inspecting tree markings and reaching to pull someone onto a wrong path.
- **waiter-v1.png / ผู้เฝ้ารอ — THE WAITER:** Families unable to let the missing leave. Broad weathered doorway forms the entire torso with an open empty threshold, no body inside. Multiple wooden hands grip the frame or reach inward, short backwards-pointing feet, rusted hardware and sparse cotton remnants. Show blocking an abandoned house exit. English working name may be revised to THE WAITING ONE to avoid the restaurant meaning of waiter.
- **binder-v1.png / ผู้ผูกไว้ — THE BINDER:** People exchanged through the fictional return ritual. Hunched asymmetric figure entirely built from lost clothing, bags, camera straps, sandals and buckles across hollow rope framework. Faceless knotted head, long looped restraint arm, bulky short arm. Muted indigo cotton, offwhite and worn leather. Show reaching and restraining. Increase clear torso gaps if the cloth silhouette looks too much like a person in costume.
- **name-eraser-v1.png / ผู้ลบชื่อ — THE NAME ERASER:** Concealed missing-person evidence. Hollow layered paper-and-ink body, compressed paper head with scraped-through oval face hole, file-divider shoulders, long paper strips, one oversized wooden rubber-stamp arm and stiff paper fingers. Offwhite, black ink and restrained red. Show guarding case files. Generated stamp glyphs are illustrative placeholders; replace them with fictional Thai document marks for production.

## Review notes

The generation emphasizes weathered material detail. For the next pass, test smaller gameplay silhouettes and reduce details that disappear at distance. Searcher and Name Eraser should differ further in posture and movement so their long limbs do not read as the same enemy. Waiter requires a custom doorway rig; start implementation experiments with Searcher rather than all four at once.
