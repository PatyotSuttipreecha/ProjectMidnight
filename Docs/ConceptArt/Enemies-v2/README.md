# Enemy visual revision v2

Built-in Imagegen concepts following user direction: more humanlike, more frightening, less fantastical object anatomy. Original v1 images are preserved. These sheets are concept exploration, not implemented enemies or final model blueprints.

## Final prompt set / visual specifications

Shared: original Project Midnight semi-realistic cinematic horror character sheets, large full-body three-quarter, smaller side/back views and anatomical closeups; readable neutral grey studio background, ordinary worn rural clothing and mottled biological skin. Clearly impossible supernatural anatomy with no living captive inside. No wooden/paper/object bodies, robots, mummies, generic zombies, copied franchise creatures or religious iconography. Uncanny body horror without excessive blood or exposed organs. No text.

- **searcher-v2.png — ผู้ค้นหา / The Searcher:** Gaunt adult field-search silhouette with weathered khaki jacket, trousers and muddy shoes. Head held sideways in a listening posture, continuous skin over eye sockets, overlong arm and fingers holding an actual worn flashlight. Endless searching conveyed by posture and real equipment rather than a flashlight head.
- **waiting-one-v2.png — ผู้เฝ้ารอ / The Waiting One:** Elderly-looking supernatural silhouette in worn indigo cotton blouse and long brown sarong, bare feet. Deep inward sealed-skin chest depression, sealed mouth, dark eye recesses, elongated arms and too many fingers locked in a cradling pose. Alternate reaching pose conveys refusing to let someone leave.
- **bound-one-v2.png — ผู้ผูกไว้ / The Bound One:** Adult male-like flesh body in offwhite cotton shirt and dark trousers. Torso twists against pelvis, asymmetric shoulders, one arm abnormally long and the other folded behind the shoulder. Sparse camera straps constrict body/clothes; distorted fused facial features and opposing movement directions. Not a restrained living captive or body made of straps.
- **name-eraser-v2.png — ผู้ลบชื่อ / The Name Eraser:** Thin worker-like silhouette in ordinary button shirt, trousers and shoes. Entire face covered by tight featureless depressed skin, asymmetric long forearm, ink-blackened elongated flat fingers with abnormal bends. Small vignette rubbing ink over a case record; papers remain props, not anatomy.

## Review observations

All four now share flesh, fabric and grounded anatomy. The Waiting One has the strongest torso/hand silhouette. Searcher and Name Eraser still share long arms and obscured faces; differentiate their movement in the next pass. Dark backgrounds in gameplay may hide the facial cues, so test creature silhouettes and lighting at actual camera distance. These images alone do not settle how the story establishes that enemies are supernatural rather than living people.
